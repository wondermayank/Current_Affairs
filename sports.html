India Clinch First Bronze at Junior Hockey World Cup — Chennai

🏆 ICC MEN'S T20 WORLD CUP 2026 · FINAL · MARCH 8

India Win Historic 3rd T20 World Cup Title — Thrash New Zealand by 96 Runs

In front of over 100,000 fans at Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad, India exorcised the demons of 2023 and won their 3rd ICC Men's T20 World Cup title. Sanju Samson (89 off fewer balls) and Abhishek Sharma (18-ball 50+) opened with 92 runs in the first 6 overs — the highest WC Final powerplay. Jasprit Bumrah then bowled 4/15 to skittle New Zealand for 159. India became the first team to defend their T20 World Cup title, the first to win it at home, and the first team to win three T20 World Cup titles. New Zealand suffered their 5th straight ICC final defeat in 11 years.
